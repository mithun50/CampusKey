Get ready before you apply

1. Activate your college email

Addresses ending in .ac.in, .edu.in or your college domain get approved fastest. Ask your IT cell or admin office if you do not have one yet.

2. Scan your ID card clearly

Take a sharp photo of the front of your ID showing your name, college name and a validity date. Many forms reject blurry or cropped images.

3. Keep a bonafide certificate

If your ID has no expiry date, a dated bonafide or fee receipt from this academic year works as proof for most programs.

4. Match your name everywhere

Use the same name on your account profile as on your ID. Mismatches are the most common reason for rejection.

Common questions

Is CampusKey affiliated with these companies?

No. CampusKey is an independent guide. We link to official pages and you always claim offers directly from the provider.

How accurate is this information?

Each offer was checked against official sources on the date shown. Companies change terms often, so always confirm the final price and eligibility on the official page before paying or submitting documents.

I do not have a college email. Can I still claim offers?

Yes. Choose "College ID card" in the filter. Programs like the GitHub Student Developer Pack, JetBrains and many others accept an ID card or enrollment document instead.

Why are Netflix, JioHotstar and news sites missing?

We checked them and found no student plan in India for Netflix, JioHotstar, JioSaavn, Gaana or Times Prime. We could not confirm student plans from The Hindu, Economic Times or Indian Express either, so we left them out. Your college library may already give you free access to newspapers and journals.

What does the confidence label mean?

High means the offer is confirmed for India on the official page. Medium means it is available but some details such as India pricing could not be fully confirmed. Low means it may change or availability in India is uncertain.

Is it safe to upload my ID card?

Only upload documents on the provider's official site or its named verification partner such as SheerID or UNiDAYS. Never send your ID over chat apps or to unofficial pages.
